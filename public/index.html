Writing and Speaking band descriptors, in plain English

BandIdeas & taskOrganisation / fluencyVocabularyGrammar
9Fully answers every part; ideas are sharp and well supported.Flows naturally; linking is invisible.Wide, precise, natural; rare slips only.Wide range, almost error-free.
8Covers all parts well; ideas extended and relevant.Logical paragraphs; speaks easily with rare hesitation.Skilful use of less common words and collocations.Most sentences error-free; varied structures.
7Clear position, main ideas extended, sometimes over-general.Clear progression; good range of linkers.Some less common words; aware of style.Many error-free complex sentences.
6Answers all parts, but some ideas undeveloped.Organised, but linking can be mechanical or repetitive.Enough words for the task; some wrong choices.Mix of simple and complex; errors rarely block meaning.
5Partly answers; ideas limited or repeated.Some organisation; overuse of the same linkers.Limited range; spelling errors noticeable.Mostly simple sentences; frequent errors.
4Answers only part of the task; position unclear.Ideas not arranged clearly; long pauses.Basic words, often repeated.Very limited; errors often confuse the reader.
